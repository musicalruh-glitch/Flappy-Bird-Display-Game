I built a hand held game, the in game name being  "Flappy Bird Soul." The game is inspired by Undertale, (like all my other Half Life projects) and it was originally supposed to be a Flappy Bird game, hence the name but it's inspired by the attack patterns in game Undertale, sharing the same sprites and music. ### **How it works:**

As I've mentioned, the game itself is heavily inspired by Undertale, every bit of it. I've had to make modifications since they're not the same thing -- obviously, but think of it as Undertale if it was a handheld Flappy-ish Bird game.

When you press the on/off button on the side, you're greeted with the title "Flappy Bird Soul" on the menu, along with a saved High Score and the prompt PRESS ANY TO START. To get started here, press any of the 4 directional buttons on the device to trigger the startup chime and launch straight into battle.

On the active gameplay screen you'll see the Undertale rectangular Battle Box, the HP: 20/20 counter in the top left, and your live SCR (score) counter in the top right. The entity you control? It's the classic soul from Undertale! It moves smoothly in 4 directions w/ no gravity (so not exactly flappy bird haha...). You'll have to dodge incoming obstacles like Papyrus's bone pillars or Toriel's fireballs, -- the two added attack patterns! Every obstacle you successfully dodge increases your score. If an obstacle hits your soul, it plays a damage sound effect and reduces your HP by 1. Once your HP is 0 you lose :(((

And the classic Game Over screen! You'll see big bold text reading "GAME OVER" accompanied by a descending chiptune shard-break sound effect, followed by a PRESS ANY TO RESET. Classic reset. When you reach this screen, as instructed press any button to save your high score and return to the main intro menu.

Usually for games like Flappy Bird there is no way to win the game (as far as I know); but that felt a bit half-baked for my taste, so if you last against the battle attacks for 10 whopping minutes,  you get the YOU WIN! display screen and the device tracks your total wins. Once you reach here, press any button to return to the menu for another run.
